FAQ

Questions people ask

How do I know if I need a writing service?

If people keep asking “wait, what does your project do?” after visiting your website, you need help. If your community is confused, you need help. If you’re getting interest but not conversions, you need help.

How soon can I expect results?

For most projects, you’ll see improvements immediately. Clearer messaging means fewer confused users. Better content means higher engagement. It’s not magic — it’s just finally making sense to people.

What platforms do you write for?

Wherever your audience is. Twitter/X, Medium, your website, Discord, Telegram, Substack — if it involves words that need to connect with humans, I can help.

How will we communicate?

Whatever works best for you: email, Telegram, Discord, calls. I’m flexible. You’ll get regular updates, and you can always reach me if you have questions.

Do you only work with big projects?

No — I work with projects of all sizes, from solo founders just starting out to established protocols. If you need help communicating clearly, we can probably work together.
